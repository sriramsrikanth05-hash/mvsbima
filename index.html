FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge for your advice?

No. Consultation with MVS Bima is free. We help you understand your options so you can make an informed decision.

How much health insurance cover do I need?

It depends on your city, age, family size and existing cover. As a starting point, many families consider at least ₹5–10 lakh. We'll help you work out a cover amount that suits your situation.

What is the difference between term insurance and other life insurance?

Term insurance is pure protection — it pays a large sum to your family if something happens to you during the policy term, at a much lower premium than savings-linked plans.

Can you help me renew my existing car or bike insurance?

Yes. Share your current policy details and we'll help you renew, compare options and suggest useful add-ons like zero depreciation.

Will you help me when I need to make a claim?

Yes. We assist with documentation and coordination with the insurance company to make the claim process as smooth as possible. Final claim decisions are made by the insurer as per policy terms.
